## How a yearly salary becomes a month and a paycheck
- **Per paycheck** = yearly pay ÷ paychecks a year (52 weekly, 26 every 2 weeks, 24 twice a month, 12 monthly).
- **Per month** (what the budget uses) = yearly pay ÷ 12. Every 2 weeks, most months have 2 paychecks
  and two months have 3: the app already offers Dave Ramsey's way, "budget on 2 paychecks a month",
  so the 3rd one is extra money in its month.
- Deductions typed per paycheck become a month the same way (× paychecks a year ÷ 12); a 401(k) as a %
  of pay follows the pay.

## Deduction types and their taxes (US)
| Type | Federal / state income tax | Social Security + Medicare |
|---|---|---|
| Traditional (pre-tax) 401(k), 403(b), 457(b) | lowers it | no |
| Roth 401(k) / 403(b) / 457(b), after-tax 401(k) | no | no |
| Medical, dental, vision; health FSA, limited-purpose FSA, dependent care FSA; HSA through payroll; commuter | lowers it | lowers it |
| Life (yours, spouse, child), AD&D, short- and long-term disability, legal, other | no (after tax) | no |
Limits warned per person: 401(k)/403(b) deferrals (pre-tax + Roth together, + catch-up at 50),
457(b) separately, all contributions incl. after-tax and the match (415(c)), HSA, health FSA, dependent care FSA.

## Overtime per paycheck
Hours of overtime on a usual paycheck × the hourly rate × 1.5 (or the multiplier). Salaried
non-exempt: the hourly rate is the yearly salary ÷ 2,080. Counted in taxes always, in the budget only
when asked (Ramsey). Federal tax: the "no tax on overtime" deduction (2025–2028) for the extra half
of time-and-a-half, up to $12,500 ($25,000 joint), less above $150,000 ($300,000 joint) of income.
